# Villagen 0.11.9 Player Notes

Main server, 2026-10-16. If you have 0.11.7 or 0.11.8 installed, the game updates itself when it starts.

- Pick one of eight characters and style hair, skin, clothes, trim colour and hats. Existing accounts choose once at their next sign-in
- Set a display name (change it once a day); it shows in chat and on name tags
- 18 expedition creatures with new looks, each with its own cries and attack sounds
- Village bag with slots, tabs and 3D previews
- Farming: visible growth stages, thirsty crops when not watered; radish, potato, tomato and corn added
- Carry 7 kinds of items in hand ('Carry' in storage); others see them too
- Today's requests on the quest board, letters from residents, buy and sell in shop windows
- New residents Boni and Sol with weekday, day-off and market-day routines
- Decorating: right-click menu on placed pieces, hang pieces on walls and ceilings or stack them, big pieces stay off doorways, bridges and roads
- Homes: room doors open as people pass; sign back in where you were, or wake up in bed after a long break; owners can lock the door or send a guest out
- Crafting: choose the kind of function (chair, lamp, box …) yourself; refused requests explain why; photo orientation is fixed
- Effects: expedition swings, hits, gathering, campfire and healing; fireflies and petals in the village; tool effects at the moment of contact
- Performance: fewer village hitches (worst frames about halved) and prop LODs restored
- Whisper requests to nearby players who are not friends (they must accept)
- Fixed: a line resent after a whisper request going to global chat; rising when standing up from a chair on a stand

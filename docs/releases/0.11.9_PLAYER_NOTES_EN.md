# Villagen 0.11.9 Player Notes

Create and decorate in your village, then head out exploring with friends.

This is a review draft. **확정 (Confirmed)** means the change is merged into the integrated build, not necessarily released. **예정 (Pending)** means integration or in-game connections are still pending, and the item may be left out of the final release.

- [예정] We're preparing a choice of 8 characters, with clothing colors and hats to make your favorite traveler your own.
- [예정] New looks for 18 expedition monster types are on the way. New looks for other monsters are also in preparation.
- [예정] We're preparing a campfire effect that restores health and stamina nearby and briefly boosts your attacks after you leave.
- [예정] We're adding visible crop growth and more satisfying watering, with radishes, potatoes, tomatoes, and corn to grow.
- [예정] We're preparing a quest board where you can choose today's requests and follow directions to help residents.
- [예정] Residents are preparing letters to thank you for helping, mark special occasions, and share news of moving in.
- [예정] We're preparing scenes of Danbi's house being built and Danbi moving in and settling into the village.
- [예정] We're preparing shops where you can talk to residents to buy, sell, or order items, depending on the shop.
- [확정] Decorating: It's easier to select placed objects to move or rotate them, with guidance to keep doorways and paths clear.
- [확정] Furniture placement: Indoors, you can put objects on other furniture and place suitable objects on walls or ceilings.
- [확정] Home visits: Room doors open as you pass, and friends walk through them to enter and leave. Hosts can lock their doors or send guests outside.
- [확정] Sound: Footsteps and everyday activities have more varied sounds, and expedition monsters have their own cries and attack sounds.
- [예정] We're preparing new sound effects for farming, shops, moving in, letters, the quest board, and campfires.
- [확정] Performance: We've reduced movement calculations for off-screen residents and made the walking of residents in view smoother.
- [확정] Fix: We've reduced cases where ordinary crafting descriptions were wrongly rejected. If a craft fails while its window is closed, reopening it shows the reason.
- [확정] Fix: Reference photos now have their orientation corrected, reducing cases where sideways photos were used for crafting.
- [확정] Fix: We've fixed a problem where resending a message after a whisper request could send it to public chat.
